🏛️ I. SEMANTIC MASKING VS THE TOXICITY OF THE SOL

Marketed in France from 1976 to 2009, Mediator (benfluorex) was prescribed as an adjuvant for the treatment of type 2 diabetes and diverted as an appetite suppressant (anorectic) for weight loss.

1. The Lie of Classification (The Deceptive Codex)

Laboratoires Servier knew that benfluorex belonged to the fenfluramine family (amphetamines).

  • Semantic cheating: To obtain marketing authorization (AMM) and 65% reimbursement by Social Security, Servier presented Mediator as a "metabolic regulator" acting on lipids and sugar.
  • Chemical concealment: Servier removed all references to the product's amphetamine structure from its official scientific documentation. Yet, once ingested, benfluorex is metabolized by the liver into norfenfluramine—the exact molecule responsible for the cardiac toxicity that had already led to the ban of Isoméride (another Servier appetite suppressant) in 1997.

2. The Clinical Reality: The Destruction of Valves (The Sol)

Norfenfluramine binds to the 5-HT2B serotonergic receptors of the heart valves.

  • The physical bug: This activation causes uncontrolled cellular proliferation (fibrosis), rendering the valves rigid and unable to close properly (valvulopathy). Patients found themselves short of breath, in severe heart failure, forced to undergo open-heart surgery to replace their valves with prosthetics, or succumbed to incurable pulmonary arterial hypertension (PAH).
