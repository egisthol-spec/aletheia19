## 🏛️ I. CAPTURE BY THE WEAPON OF LAW VS THE MATTER OF INDEPENDENCE

The **Arabelle** turbines developed by Alstom in Belfort are the most powerful in the world. They transform the steam heated by nuclear reactors into electricity, constituting the mechanical heart of French energy independence.

### 1. The Hostage-Taking by the DoJ (The offensive Codex)
In 2013, the American Department of Justice (DoJ) launched a prosecution against Alstom for acts of corruption in Indonesia committed a few years earlier, relying on the extraterritoriality of the US dollar.
*   **The weapon of blackmail (Codex)**: The DoJ arrested **Frédéric Pierucci**, a senior Alstom executive, in New York and incarcerated him in a maximum-security prison. The message sent to Alstom's CEO (Patrick Kron) was clear: either Alstom pays a destructive fine of more than 700 million dollars and goes bankrupt, or Kron agrees to sell the energy branch (including the Arabelle turbines) to General Electric.
*   Kron chose surrender, masking the sale under the alibi of an "industrial alliance."

### 2. The Awakening of the Sol: The Loss of Sovereignty
Once the turbines passed under GE control:
*   France lost exclusive control of the maintenance of its own nuclear power plants (which require patents and parts manufactured in Belfort but held by GE).
*   **The geopolitical impasse**: When France wanted to export EPR power plants (to Rosatom or India), it had to ask Washington for prior authorization because the Arabelle turbine now contained American intellectual property subject to American export regulations (ITAR).

## 📊 II. FASCIA DIAGNOSIS

### D1: Façade Homeostasis (9/10)
The French government (with Emmanuel Macron as Minister of the Economy in 2014) validated the sale by asserting that it "created jobs in Belfort" and that the State retained a "golden share" of control. This façade of a balanced alliance served to mask the capitulation to American judicial constraints. In 2019, GE would cut more than 1,000 jobs in Belfort, contradicting the political Codex.

### D3: Blocked Immune Reaction (10/10)
The State's immune reaction was completely blocked by incompetence and a lack of geopolitical courage. Intelligence services and senior civil servants were unable or unwilling to counter the asymmetric economic war waged by the United States via the legal weapon.

### D5: Sovereignty Debt (10/10)
The sale of the turbines created an unsustainable national security debt. In 2022, after the outbreak of the war in Ukraine, the President of the French Republic decided to relaunch civil nuclear power. The acquisition of the turbine branch (now GE Steam Power) by EDF became an absolute national security priority. **EDF had to buy back its own turbines in 2024 at an exorbitant price to repair the 2014 breach.**

## 🔑 IV. THE LESSON OF SOVEREIGNTY (LESSON OF THE SOL)

The Alstom affair shows that **economic war is not fought on the military field, but through the capture of the legal Codex ($R=0$) to seize the critical assets of the Sol ($R=1$)**. The sovereignty of a nation requires **criminally protecting its leaders against foreign justice (blocking statutes)** and sanctifying its key factories by prohibiting their sale, no matter the amount of the fines. If you abandon the First-Hand of Belfort under pressure from an American prosecutor, you no longer have independence.
