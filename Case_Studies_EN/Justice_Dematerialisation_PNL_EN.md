## 🏛️ I. THE SOMATIC PRESENCE OF THE SOL VS THE SCREEN OF THE CODEX

Human justice is built on physicality: a place (the court), a decor (the robe, the podium), and above all, the co-presence of bodies. This somatic geometry allows for the exchange of subtle non-verbal signals (breathing rhythm, gaze, posture) essential to the evaluation of truth.

### 1. Permanent Videoconferencing (The Anesthetic Screen)
Under the guise of reducing transport costs and administrative modernization:
*   Immediate appearances and pre-trial detention hearings are increasingly conducted via **low-quality video links**.
*   **The sedation effect of the screen**: Judicial psychology studies show that a judge convicts an individual appearing on a CRT screen more severely and with less empathy than if they were seated two meters away in the courtroom. The screen disembodies the human tragedy, transforming it into an interchangeable video feed to be quickly liquidated ($R=0$).

### 2. The Illusion of Predictive Justice (The statistical Codex)
The deployment of "predictive justice" software seeks to suggest to judges the ideal "average" sentence by analyzing thousands of similar past decisions.
*   **The biophysical flaw**: AI does not judge the present case. It repeats and averages the historical biases encoded in the database. It deprives the judge of their primary function: **the cognitive apnea required to appreciate the uniqueness of an individual's physical situation**.

## 📊 II. FASCIA DIAGNOSIS

### D1: Façade Homeostasis (9/10)
The Ministry of Justice maintains the façade of "modernizing access to justice" and "reducing waiting times." This administrative homeostasis masks the fact that lawyers and litigants are excluded from the physical process, relegated to dialog boxes on internet portals (like Portalis) that crash regularly.

### D5: Oxygen Debt / Contactless Dematerialization (10/10)
Dematerialization eliminates any Fallow space or informal discussion (the corridors of the court where amicable agreements or moderate requisitions were negotiated). By forcing the transition to an uninterrupted digital flow, justice asphyxiates under the volume of illegible PDF documents pushed to overheated magistrates.

## 🔑 IV. THE LESSON OF SOVEREIGNTY (LESSON OF THE SOL)

The dematerialization of justice shows that **law is not word processing ($R=0$); it is a somatic act of sovereign responsibility ($R=1$)**. The citizen's sovereignty facing the judicial machine consists of **systematically demanding physical presence before their judge**. The justice operator must refuse the sedation of the screen and defend the courtroom as the last sanctuary of human First-Hand experience, where a person's freedom and life are decided eye to eye, under the constraint of the living.
