### 1. Act I: The Asymmetrical Inoculation (1999-2005)
In 1999, Nissan was on the verge of bankruptcy, weighed down by a debt of over 20 billion dollars. Renault injected 5.4 billion dollars to acquire 36.8% of the capital (which later rose to 44.3%). The media honeymoon began with the drastic restructuring operated by Carlos Ghosn.
* **The Structural Flaw**: The initial transaction founded a **non-superimposable capitalistic chirality**. Renault held 44.3% of Nissan with full voting rights. Nissan, once recovered, would hold only 15% of Renault with zero voting rights. The illusion of an "alliance of equals" was proclaimed for public relations purposes, but the legal structure coded a unilateral filiation relationship.

### 2. Act II: The Era of Osmotic Extraction (2005-2015)
Once recovered, Nissan became the main engine of profit and volume for the alliance. Yet, the governance structure remained unchanged.
* **The Theft of Material Stock**: During this decade, Renault was structurally in deficit or stagnating in the European market. To mask its operational weaknesses and finance its own R&D, Renault extracted a cumulative total of **4.5 billion euros in dividends** from Nissan.
* Nissan, which accounted for over 60% of sales and held the key technologies (notably in hybrids and 4x4s), saw its resources siphoned towards France with no reciprocity of control. Internally, to appease Japanese engineers, management invented semantic concepts like *"agile scale synergies"* and *"cross platforms"*.

### 3. Act III: The Sovereign State Shock (2015-2018)
The thermodynamic breaking point occurred when the political "res publica" brutally interfered with the illusion of the alliance.
* **The Florange Law Maneuver (2015)**: Seeking to guarantee the State's long-term influence in French companies, Emmanuel Macron (then Minister of the Economy) orchestrated the secret purchase by the French State of an additional 4.7% of Renault for 1.2 billion euros. This maneuver allowed the State to reach the double-voting threshold at the Renault general assembly, locking in its influence over the French manufacturer, and by extension, over Nissan.
* **Tokyo's Immune Reaction**: For the Nissan board in Tokyo and the METI (Japanese Ministry of Economy), the red line was crossed. Nissan refused to be indirectly controlled by the French State. Hiroto Saikawa and the Nissan establishment secretly activated a legal defense cell to counter the total merger plans desired by Paris and Carlos Ghosn.

### 4. Act IV: The Expulsion of the Graft and Laminarization (2018-2024)
Carlos Ghosn's arrest at Haneda airport in November 2018 was merely the legal detonator of a structural rupture that had already been consummated.
* **The Immediate Purge**: Within 24 hours of the arrest, Nissan stripped Ghosn of his powers. In the following weeks, the Japanese operational apparatus proceeded to evict all key executives attached to the French management (including Chief Performance Officer José Muñoz and several financial directors).
* **The Return to Symmetry (2024)**: After six years of a value-destroying trench war, thermodynamic reality imposed itself. In 2024, Renault agreed to reduce its stake in Nissan back to **15%**, placing the remainder in a trust to be sold, thus re-establishing an exact 15/15 parity without unilateral voting rights. Sovereign equilibrium was finally restored by the separation of the membranes.
